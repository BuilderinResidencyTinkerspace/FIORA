# Week 8

**Goal this week:**

Our main goal this week was to continue working on the **LiDAR mapping and SLAM setup** for FIORA. We also planned to improve the mapping accuracy, add delivery points to the Telegram bot, and work towards enabling automatic delivery to the selected destination. Along with this,starting to print our 3d model. 

## What we did
With the help of Devadath, we started SLAM-based mapping. We mapped different places and saved each map for future use.
During this process, the Raspberry Pi kept turning off unexpectedly. We thought the problem was with the power supply because we were using a power bank that could not provide enough current (amps) to meet the Raspberry Pi's power needs. Because of this, the Raspberry Pi kept shutting down, which made our mapping work difficult.

This week, Shan conducted a BIR sprint to help all the teams make progress on their projects. Everyone worked together and helped each other. We worked not only on our own project but also helped with other teams' projects. Through this, we got to learn many new things, and it was also a lot of fun.

After helping the other teams, we returned to FIORA and continued working on our project. We finished our design and showed it to Shan. That was when we got a big shock. He told us that we needed to make the design smaller because it was at least 4 feet tall and Printing a model that big would take several weeks and use a lot of filament.

So, we had to reduce the size of the design. This meant changing many parts of our existing model, which was a big setback for us. We now had to work on the design again while also getting the mapping ready. At the same time, our final deadline was getting closer, and we didn't have much time left. So, we got back to work and started figuring out how to manage everything before the deadline.

## Problems and blockers
* The Raspberry Pi kept shutting down during mapping, possibly because the power bank could not supply enough current.

* Our 3D model was too large to print within the available time and would require a lot of filament.

* Redesigning the model while continuing the mapping work was difficult because our final deadline was getting closer.

## Decisions

* We decided to reduce the size of our 3D model and redesign the necessary parts.

* We continued working on SLAM mapping while making plans to improve the design.

* We focused on completing the important tasks before the final deadline.

## Next week
* Continue working on SLAM mapping and improve the mapping setup.

* Redesign the 3D model to make it smaller and easier to print.

* Continue working on the Telegram bot and delivery points.

* Start printing the redesigned model if it is ready.
